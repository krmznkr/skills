# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participation should make the project easier and safer for other people to use,
understand, and improve. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable
feedback, and discuss the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, publishing another person's private information, or knowingly
introducing malicious code or misleading documentation.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces, as well as when someone represents the project in
public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not litigate sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Use the
repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel in the Security tab and
identify the report as a code-of-conduct matter. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when needed to protect the project community.

Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation
against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
